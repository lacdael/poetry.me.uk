# Returns Policy

**Last updated: July 20, 2025**

This policy outlines the terms for returns for products purchased from poetry.me.uk.

## Digital Books

Our digital books are offered as free downloads. As these items are provided at no cost, we do not offer returns, exchanges, or refunds for them.

## Hardcopy Books

We are committed to ensuring our customers are satisfied with their purchases. If you have purchased a hardcopy book and it arrives damaged or with a manufacturing defect, you may be eligible for a replacement or a full refund.

### Return Process

To request a return for a hardcopy book, please follow these steps:

1.  **Contact Us:** Email us at **c.lacdael@gmail.co.uk** within 30 days of receiving your order.
2.  **Provide Details:** In your email, please include:
    *   Your full name and order number.
    *   A clear explanation of the reason for the return (e.g., damaged during shipping, printing error).
    *   **A photograph** of the damaged or defective item. A picture is required to process your return request.

### Review

Once we receive your email and photo, we will review your request. We will notify you of the approval or rejection of your return. If your return is approved, we will provide instructions for the next steps, whether it's a replacement or a refund.

### Questions

If you have any questions about our Returns Policy, please do not hesitate to contact us at **c.lacdael@gmail.co.uk**.
